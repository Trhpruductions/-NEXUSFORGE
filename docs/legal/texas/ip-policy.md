## 4. Copyright complaints: DMCA notice and takedown

4.1 We respect copyright. We respond to copyright complaints under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act, 17 U.S.C. § 512.

4.2 **How to send a copyright notice.** Send copyright notices through the Support page at https://nfgcord.com/support, with the subject line **"Copyright notice"**.

4.3 A copyright notice must be in writing and include each of the following elements (17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3)(A)):

- (a) **Signature.** A physical or electronic signature of the person authorised to act for the owner of the copyright.
- (b) **The work.** Identification of the copyrighted work that you say has been infringed or, if several works are covered by one notice, a representative list of them.
- (c) **The material.** Identification of the material that you say is infringing, and information reasonably sufficient for us to find it, such as the web address, or the forge, channel, message or file identifiers. Please be as specific as you can.
- (d) **Your contact details.** Your name, postal address, telephone number and email address.
- (e) **Good-faith statement.** A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent or the law.
- (f) **Accuracy and authority statement.** A statement that the information in the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are authorised to act on behalf of the owner of the exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.

4.4 **Incomplete notices.** A notice that leaves out these elements may not be effective under the law. We may contact you to ask for the missing information, and we are not required to act on an incomplete notice.

4.5 **What we do.** When we receive a valid notice, we will act expeditiously to remove or disable access to the material. Where we can, we will tell the person who posted it. We may also take action under the Moderation Policy.

4.6 **Misrepresentation.** Under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f), a person who knowingly and materially misrepresents that material is infringing, or that material was removed by mistake or misidentification, may be liable for damages, including costs and attorneys' fees. Please consider the law carefully before you send a notice.

## 5. Counter-notices

5.1 If we remove or disable material because of a notice, the person who posted it may send us a counter-notice under 17 U.S.C. § 512(g).

5.2 A counter-notice must be in writing and include:

- (a) a physical or electronic signature;
- (b) identification of the material that was removed or disabled, and the location where it appeared before it was removed;
- (c) a statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of a mistake or misidentification of the material; and
- (d) your name, address and telephone number, a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for the district where your address is located (or, if your address is outside the United States, any judicial district in which we may be found), and a statement that you will accept service of process from the person who sent the original notice.

5.3 Send the counter-notice through the Support page at https://nfgcord.com/support, with the subject line **"Copyright notice"**, and say at the start of the message that it is a counter-notice.

5.4 **What we do.** We will forward the counter-notice to the person who sent the original notice. Unless that person tells us, within the period the law allows, that they have filed a court action to restrain you from the activity, we may restore the material or stop disabling access to it. We will do this within not less than 10 and not more than 14 business days after we receive your counter-notice.

5.5 Restoring material does not affect any other action that we may take under the Moderation Policy or the Terms of Service.

## 7. Trademark complaints

7.1 The Marks are owned by TRH Development, a sole proprietorship owned and operated by Hank.

7.2 Trademark rights are separate from copyright, so the DMCA procedure in section 4 does not apply to them. Federal trademark law is in the Lanham Act, including 15 U.S.C. §§ 1114 and 1125. The DMCA does not provide a trademark notice procedure, so we handle trademark complaints under this section and under the Terms of Service.

7.3 **How to make a trademark complaint.** Send the complaint through the Support page at https://nfgcord.com/support, with the subject line **"Trademark complaint"**. Include the following:

- (a) your name, postal address and email address, and, if you act for someone else, the name of the trademark owner and your authority to act for it;
- (b) the mark that you say is being infringed, and whether it is registered (including the country or state and the registration number);
- (c) the location of the content or account, such as a web address, forge name or user name;
- (d) an explanation of how the content is likely to cause confusion, or how it uses the mark without permission;
- (e) a statement that you believe the use is not authorised; and
- (f) a statement that the information in the complaint is accurate and, if you act for the owner, that you are authorised to do so.

7.4 **Our review.** We review each complaint against the Terms of Service, the Acceptable Use Policy and the Community Rules. We may remove content, ask for changes, ask for more information, or take no action. We reply as soon as we can. We cannot give legal advice to complainants or users.

7.5 **References to the name.** Not every mention of the Nexus Forge Gaming name is an infringement. Factual references, news, reviews, commentary and criticism are generally treated differently from use that suggests that we endorse, sponsor or are affiliated with a person, forge or product.
