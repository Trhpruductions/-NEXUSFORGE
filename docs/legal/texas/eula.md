## 4. Automatic updates

4.1 **What updates do.** The App checks for new versions when it starts and about every 15 minutes while it is open. When it finds a newer version, it downloads that version in the background. On Windows, the App installs a downloaded update when you close it. The next time you open the App, you are running the new version.

4.2 **Prompts.** When the App checks for updates on its own, it does not show a prompt. If you check for updates yourself, the App shows a notice where you can start the download now, choose "Remind Me Later", or view what is new. "Remind Me Later" waits one hour before the reminder returns. When an update has finished downloading, the App may also ask you to restart now or install later.

4.3 **Required updates.** We may mark an update as required, for example to fix a security problem, to meet a legal requirement or to keep the Services working. For a required update, the App installs the downloaded update and restarts without waiting for you to close it. A restart can interrupt a call, a stage or other activity in progress.

4.4 **Using the App after a required update.** We may require you to install a required update before you keep using the App, or certain features of it. If you do not install it, we may limit or stop your access to the App or to those features until you update.

4.5 **Postponing updates.** If an update is not required, you may choose "Remind Me Later" when you check for updates yourself. The App's desktop settings do not include an option to turn off update checks.

4.6 **Changes through updates.** An update may change the App's features, appearance or behaviour. Updates are part of the App, and this License Agreement applies to them, unless an update comes with its own terms, which we will show you.
