# Nexus Forge Gaming Community Rules

**Draft pending attorney review. This document is not final.**

Version 1.0 (draft)
Effective date: the date the operator publishes this document
Operator: TRH Development, a sole proprietorship owned and operated by Hank

---

## The short version

- **Treat people with respect.** No harassment, threats or hate.
- **Keep private things private.** Don't share someone's personal details, DMs or private calls.
- **Keep it 18+ and safe for everyone.** Nothing sexual involving anyone under 18, ever. Adult sexual content is not allowed.
- **No scams.** Never ask for passwords or codes, and don't sell accounts or Coins for money.
- **Play fair.** No spam, raids, cheating or exploiting the economy.
- **Be yourself.** Don't pretend to be someone else or to be staff.
- **Ask before recording.** Don't record or share someone's voice or video without their consent.
- **Report what breaks the rules.** Moderators must enforce the rules fairly.

---

## 1. Who these rules are for

These Community Rules apply in every Forge, channel, thread, forum post, direct message, voice room, stage, event, feed post and comment on Nexus Forge Gaming ("Nexus Forge Gaming," "we," "us," or "our"). They work alongside the Acceptable Use Policy, which holds the full list of prohibited conduct, and the Terms of Service. A Forge owner can add rules for their own Forge, but those rules cannot allow what these rules forbid.

Capitalized terms have the meanings given in the Terms of Service.

## 2. The rules

### 2.1 Be respectful

Disagree with ideas, not people. Friendly trash talk among players who are all in on the joke is fine. Wearing someone down is not.

- **Okay:** "Your aim is rough. Want a tip?"
- **Not okay:** Repeatedly insulting someone after they have asked you to stop, or sending the same insult across several channels.

### 2.2 No harassment, threats or hate

Don't target, stalk, intimidate or threaten anyone. Don't attack people for who they are, including their religion, race, sexuality, gender, disability or other protected characteristics.

- **Okay:** Calling out a bad play and moving on.
- **Not okay:** "Everyone like you should get banned from every forge." Attacking someone for their religion or sexuality, even as a joke.
- **Not okay:** "I know where you live." Any threat is a serious violation, even if you say you were joking.

### 2.3 Keep private information private

Don't post someone's real name, home address, phone number, school, workplace, private photos or identity documents, even if they upset you. Don't try to find out someone's IP address through a call, a link or a file. Some voice connections can show your IP address to other people on the call, so think about that before you join.

- **Not okay:** "I'll post where she works." This is a threat, not a joke.
- **Not okay:** Sharing a screenshot of a DM to embarrass someone.

### 2.4 Keep it safe for minors

The Services are for people aged 18 and over. Don't sexualize anyone who may be under 18, whether the person is real or drawn. Don't ask anyone's age, school, location, photos or contact details to "check" whether they are an adult.

If you think someone may be under 18, stop the conversation, don't ask for ID or photos, and report it.

- **Not okay:** Flirting with a player who says they're still in high school.
- **Okay:** Ending the conversation and reporting the player.

### 2.5 No sexual content

Adult sexual content is not allowed on Nexus Forge Gaming. That includes sexually explicit images, video, audio, text, links, avatars, banners, profile pictures and streams. Flirting or jokes that are not explicit are still covered by the Respect and Minors rules above.

### 2.6 No threats, violence or encouraging self-harm

Don't threaten or encourage violence, and don't praise violent attackers or extremist groups.

If someone says they might hurt themselves, don't joke about it. Tell a moderator or report the message. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first. In the United States, you can call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

### 2.7 No scams, phishing or account deals

Never ask for or share passwords, sign-in codes or recovery codes, including with someone who says they work for Nexus Forge Gaming. Don't sell, rent or trade accounts or access. Don't sell Coins, items or jackpot tickets for money. Don't post fake giveaways or links that promise free Coins.

- **Not okay:** A link to a "verification" page that asks you to log in to claim free Coins.
- **Not okay:** "Trading 10,000 Coins for PayPal, DM me."

### 2.8 No spam or raids

Don't flood channels, repeat the same message, spam links or invites, mass-mention people, or send unsolicited messages in bulk. Don't organize groups to attack or disrupt a Forge, channel, event or voice room.

Use bots only as their rules allow. Don't run self-bots or auto-clickers on your own account.

### 2.9 Play fair

Don't cheat the rewards. Don't use scripts or multiple accounts to farm daily rewards, mining income or activity. Don't exploit bugs. Report them to support instead.

Don't try to manipulate or predict jackpot draws. Don't run a prize draw, raffle or giveaway in which people pay, or give something of value, to enter. Don't use the Services to promote gambling or betting.

### 2.10 Don't pretend to be someone else

Don't impersonate other players, creators, brands, moderators or Nexus Forge Gaming staff. Don't use a display name or avatar that is meant to look like an official account.

A clearly labeled fan account or parody, which doesn't trick anyone, is fine. Copyright rules still apply.

### 2.11 Respect other people's content

Only post what you have the right to share. Credit creators when you share their work. Copyright and trademark matters are handled under the Intellectual Property Policy.

### 2.12 Profiles, the feed and events

- Profile pictures, banners and avatars follow the same rules, including the rule against adult sexual content.
- Feed posts and comments follow the same rules as chat. Don't harass people in comments, spam the feed, or share images of people without their consent.
- Only share clips of other people with their consent. Don't clip private conversations.
- Event hosts are responsible for their events. Don't use an event to run a scam, collect personal data, or advertise outside the Services without permission.

## 3. Voice, video, stages and screen sharing

- **Ask before recording.** Don't record, screenshot or broadcast someone else's voice or video without their consent.
- **Keep calls private.** Don't share another person's private conversation, messages or details from a call.
- **Don't stream others' voice chat.** Don't stream a voice room, call or stage outside the Services unless everyone who can be heard has agreed.
- **Screen sharing.** Don't show private information such as passwords, identity documents, private messages or other people's details. Don't show anything that breaks these rules.
- **No disruptive sound.** Don't blast loud or disruptive sounds, or use the soundboard to harass people.
- **Respect minors in voice.** Keep conversations appropriate for everyone who may be listening. Nothing sexual or romantic may be said to anyone who may be under 18. If you hear it, report it and leave the call if you need to.
- **Stages.** Speakers are responsible for what they say and show. Stage hosts and moderators can remove speakers and listeners who break these rules.
- **No recording by the Services.** The Services do not record or transcribe voice or video calls.

## 5. Forge owners and moderators

Forge owners and moderators have extra duties. They must enforce these rules in their Forges, fairly and consistently.

**5.1 Your duties.**

- Write Forge rules that match these Community Rules and the Acceptable Use Policy. Forge rules can be stricter, but they cannot allow what these rules forbid.
- Use the lightest action that fits the problem. Give warnings for minor issues.
- Keep your decisions visible in the Forge audit log.
- Set AutoMod filters for the Forge's needs. Don't set filters meant to catch one particular person.
- Review reports from your channels promptly. Escalate serious matters to Nexus Forge Gaming through the Support page.
- Keep the bots and webhooks in your Forge within their rules. You are responsible for them.

**5.2 You must never use your powers to:**

- harass, intimidate or target someone;
- punish people for disagreeing with you, criticizing you or taking part in a personal dispute;
- settle personal scores, or remove or ban rivals or former partners;
- collect personal information about members beyond what the rules need;
- take Coins, items or Forge settings for yourself or for others; or
- hide your own misconduct or hide reports about it.

**5.3 Consequences.** Misusing moderator powers can lead to losing moderator access or removal from a Forge, and to action under the Acceptable Use Policy and the Moderation Policy. Nexus Forge Gaming may take action against a Forge owner or moderator who misuses their powers.

## 6. If you break these rules

- **Forge moderators** can warn you, delete your messages, timeout you, kick you or ban you from their Forge.
- **Nexus Forge Gaming** can warn you, remove content, restrict features, suspend your account or permanently ban it. The Acceptable Use Policy and the Moderation Policy explain these steps.

**Appeals.** You can appeal a decision made by Nexus Forge Gaming under the Appeals Policy. Decisions made by a Forge's owners or moderators are handled first within that Forge. If you believe a Forge moderator broke these rules or the Acceptable Use Policy, tell us through the Support page at https://nfgcord.com/support.

---

## 7. Reporting

- On a message or a profile, choose Report and pick the closest reason. Reports about channel messages go to that Forge's moderators. Reports about direct messages go to Nexus Forge Gaming staff.
- Don't report someone to harass them, and don't retaliate against anyone who reported you.
- We don't tell the person you reported who reported them.

If you can't sign in, use the Report a problem form on the Support page.
