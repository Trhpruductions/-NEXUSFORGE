# Nexus Forge Gaming Appeals Policy

**Draft pending attorney review. This document is not final.**

Version 1.0 (draft)
Effective date: the date the operator publishes this document
Operator: TRH Development, a sole proprietorship owned and operated by Hank

---

## 1. Who can appeal

**1.1** Any user who is subject to a decision made by Nexus Forge Gaming staff ("Nexus Forge Gaming," "we," "us," or "our") can appeal that decision. This covers:

- removal of your Content;
- a restriction on your account or on a feature;
- a suspension or a permanent ban;
- removal of Coins or items under the Acceptable Use Policy;
- removal of a Forge you own, or of your control over it; and
- a rejected age verification.

**1.2** Only the account holder can appeal. If you believe someone else accessed your account and caused the decision, say so in your appeal. We always review that claim (see Section 3.6).

---

## 2. How to appeal

**2.1 If you are signed in.** Open the Support page at https://nfgcord.com/support, or Support inside the app. Open a support ticket, choose the "Account & sign-in" category, and describe the decision you are appealing.

**2.2 If your account is suspended.** A suspended account cannot sign in or use the Services, so it cannot open a support ticket. Instead, use the Report a problem form on the Support page at https://nfgcord.com/support. Choose "Can't sign in" or "Something else," say that you are appealing a suspension, and, if you want a reply, enter an email address where we can reach you. The suspension notice and the suspended-account page both link to this Policy.

**2.3 Timing.** Send your appeal as soon as you can after the decision. An appeal sent long after the decision may not be reviewed, particularly where the relevant records are no longer available.

**2.4 One appeal per decision.** Send one appeal for each decision. Appeals that repeat an earlier appeal without new information are not reviewed again.

**2.5 What to include.** Your appeal should include:

- which decision you are appealing, and when it was made, if you know;
- why you think the decision was wrong, including any context we may have missed;
- any new information or evidence that supports your appeal;
- whether you think someone else used your account; and
- only the personal information of other people that is needed to explain your appeal.

Never send your password, sign-in codes or recovery codes. We do not need them to review an appeal. Do not send identity documents unless we ask for them. Age-verification documents are handled under the Privacy Policy.

---

## 3. What this Policy does not cover

**3.1 Forge decisions.** Decisions made by a Forge's owners or moderators are covered by Section 4, not by the rest of this Policy.

**3.2 Automated Forge filters.** Actions taken by AutoMod under a Forge's own rules are handled by that Forge's owners and moderators, as described in Section 4.

**3.3 Reports closed without action.** A decision to take no action on a report you made is not an appeal decision under this Policy. If you think a report was handled wrongly, tell us through the Support page.

**3.4 Sexual content and child safety.** We do not hear appeals against decisions about sexual content, including adult sexual content, or about child safety, except where the law requires a review process. Section 3.6 still applies.

**3.5 Legal requirements.** A decision that follows a valid legal order, or a report that the law requires us to make, is not reviewed under this Policy.

**3.6 Compromised accounts.** A claim that someone else accessed your account to cause a decision is always reviewed, whatever kind of decision it was.

**3.7 Jackpot outcomes.** Outcomes of jackpot rounds are handled under the Virtual Currency Terms, not under this Policy.

---

## 4. Decisions made within a Forge

**4.1** Forge owners and moderators make decisions within their own Forge, such as deleting a message, timing a member out, kicking or banning them. Contact the Forge's owner or moderators first.

**4.2** If you believe a Forge moderator broke the Community Rules or the Acceptable Use Policy, for example by harassing you or misusing moderation tools, tell us through the Support page at https://nfgcord.com/support. We review those complaints and act where a moderator has broken the rules. We do not reverse a Forge decision simply because you disagree with it.

---

## 5. How we review appeals

**5.1 Who reviews.** Where possible, a staff member who did not make the original decision reviews the appeal. Appeals about permanent bans or serious matters may be reviewed by a more senior staff member.

**5.2 What the reviewer looks at.** The reviewer looks at the original record, the content and its context, the notice you were given, your appeal and any new information. The reviewer also checks whether the decision followed the Moderation Policy.

**5.3 Timing.** We aim to decide appeals as promptly as we can. If a review will take longer than we expected, we will tell you.

**5.4 Replies.** We reply through the route you used: in the app for signed-in users, or to the email address you gave us through the Report a problem form.

---

## 6. Possible outcomes

- **Upheld.** The decision stands. We tell you why.
- **Changed.** The action is reduced or changed, for example a suspension is shortened or a permanent ban becomes a temporary restriction.
- **Reversed.** The decision is withdrawn. We restore Content, access, and any Coins or items removed because of the decision, where our records allow.

An appeal decision is final, unless you send important new information that was not available when the appeal was decided. In that case, we may review the matter again.

Whatever the outcome, the record of the decision is kept in the moderation audit trail. A reversed decision is marked as reversed.

---

## 7. While your appeal is pending

**7.1** Removed Content stays removed while the appeal is reviewed.

**7.2** Temporary restrictions and suspensions stay in place unless the reviewer lifts them.

**7.3** Permanent bans stay in place.

**7.4** Coins and items linked to the decision stay as they are until the appeal is decided, unless the reviewer decides otherwise.

**7.5** Where a safety risk remains, restrictions can continue during the review.

**7.6** Do not create a new account to get around a restriction. That breaks the Acceptable Use Policy and can make an appeal harder to grant.

---

## 8. Explanations and rights under Texas privacy law

**8.1 Automated decisions.** Suspensions and bans on the Services are applied by Nexus Forge Gaming staff. Automated tools such as AutoMod act only on rules that a Forge owner has set. Where a decision was made by automated means and the law gives you a right to an explanation of it, ask us through the Support page. We will explain the main reasons for the decision, the kind of information we used, and how you can exercise any further right that the law gives you.

**8.2 Refused privacy requests.** Under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (Tex. Bus. & Com. Code ch. 541), you can ask us to act on certain requests about your personal data. If we decline a request, the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act gives you a right to appeal that refusal. Our response will explain how to make the appeal and how to contact the Texas Attorney General if the refusal is upheld.

**8.3 Texas Attorney General.** You can make a complaint to the Consumer Protection Division of the Office of the Texas Attorney General. Current contact details are on the Attorney General's website.

---

## 9. Other rights and disputes

Using this Policy does not take away any right you have under the law. An appeal under this Policy is an internal review. The dispute provisions of the Terms of Service, including any arbitration terms and venue provisions, apply where they apply.

---

## 10. Abusive and repeated appeals

We may close an appeal that is abusive, that harasses staff, that repeats an earlier appeal without new information, or that is sent through several routes to pressure staff. We will tell you if we do.

---

## 11. Records and privacy

The information you send with an appeal is used under the Privacy Policy and the Moderation Policy. The appeal and its outcome are kept in the moderation record described in Section 10 of the Moderation Policy.
