## 2. Personal data we collect

### 2.1 Account information

When you create an account we collect your username, email address, password and date of birth. We store your password only as a salted one-way hash. You may also add a phone number, which we verify by text message, and two-factor sign-in by authenticator app, email or text message. An authenticator secret is stored in encrypted form, and recovery codes are stored only as one-way hashes.

Your profile can include a display name, bio, profile picture, banner, clan tag, social links, status and avatar designs.

### 2.2 Content you create

- messages, reactions, polls, pins and threads in Forges and channels;
- direct messages, friends and follows;
- feed posts, comments, likes and short clips;
- events, support tickets, problem reports, user reports and appeals; and
- uploads: profile pictures, Forge icons and banners, and message attachments. Pictures must be PNG, JPEG or WebP files.

When you report content, we keep a copy of the reported message so that it can still be reviewed if it is later deleted. The person you report is not told who reported them.

### 2.3 Purchases and virtual items

- **Subscriptions and other purchases.** Core+ plans are billed through Stripe, monthly or yearly. Other purchases are made through the same billing system. We keep your plan status and purchase records. Our Refund Policy explains purchases and cancellations.
- **Payment cards.** Stripe collects and handles your card details. We do not receive or store your full card number.
- **Coins and items.** We keep your Coin balance and transaction history, including rewards, mining income, cosmetic purchases, jackpot tickets and any transfers between users where that feature is available. We also keep records of the items you own, your mining rig progress and your jackpot entries.

### 2.4 Age information

We keep your declared date of birth, the outcome of any age check, and the date of the check. If you submit an identity document, we also handle it, and any selfie, as described in Section 5. The age gate sets a cookie, described in Section 9.

### 2.5 Technical and device information

- **IP addresses** are used for security, rate limiting, sign-in alerts and abuse prevention. The form depends on the record:
  - session records keep only the network prefix of the address, for example 203.0.113.x;
  - request logs keep a truncated, salted hash of the address;
  - audit records for some actions keep the full address, and others keep a salted hash; and
  - age-gate audit records keep the full address and the user agent.
- **Device and browser information**, such as the browser or app, operating system, user agent and language.
- **Sessions and sign-in history**, such as when you signed in, when a session was last used, and a device label such as "Chrome on Windows." You can see and sign out of your sessions in Settings.
- **Logs.** Error and audit logs help us keep the Services running and secure. Staff with admin access can see them.
- **Push details.** If you turn on push notifications, your browser gives us a push address for your device.
- **Security signals**, including rate limit counters and signals used to detect automated or abusive activity.
- **Browser error reports.** When an error occurs in the web app, the app sends the error message, stack trace and page path to our own server.

### 2.6 Information generated by the Services

We create information about your activity, including your online status and when you were last seen, notifications, rankings, achievements, moderation records, and audit records of administrative actions.

### 2.7 Information from other sources

- **Other users** may mention you, add you to a conversation, or report content involving you.
- **Discord.** If you link a Discord account, we receive your Discord user ID and what is needed to confirm the link. We do not receive your Discord password. See Section 11.
- **Stripe** tells us whether a payment or subscription succeeded, and its status.

### 2.8 Information we do not collect

- **Precise location.** The web app and desktop app do not request your device's location.
- **Biometric identifiers.** We do not capture retina or iris scans, fingerprints, voiceprints or records of face geometry. See Section 10.
- **Advertising and tracking.** The Services do not include third-party advertising, analytics or tracking libraries or scripts.

## 3. Where your information comes from

We collect personal data from you; from your devices and browser; from other users; from Discord and Stripe, as described in Section 2.7; from the sites you share, when our server fetches them to build a link preview (Section 6.1); and from our own systems, which create records about activity, rewards, security and moderation.

## 4. Why we use personal data

- **To provide the Services**: your account, messages and files, Forges, voice and video, the feed, events, Coins, purchases and other features.
- **To keep accounts secure**: protecting passwords and sessions, sending sign-in and verification codes, alerting you to sign-ins from new devices, supporting two-factor sign-in, limiting repeated requests, and investigating incidents.
- **To verify age**: confirming that users are 18 or older, unlocking features that require a verified adult, and keeping records of age decisions (Section 5).
- **To process payments**: taking payments, managing subscriptions and the billing portal, and keeping purchase records for accounting and fraud prevention.
- **To communicate with you**: transactional email, text messages containing codes, security and account notices, support replies, and the notifications you choose.
- **To keep the community safe**: applying our Terms of Service, Acceptable Use Policy and Community Rules, reviewing reports and appeals, applying AutoMod filters that a Forge has turned on, and preventing fraud, spam and abuse.
- **To operate and improve the Services**: troubleshooting, understanding feature use in aggregate, planning capacity and fixing errors. Section 11 describes the alerts we receive.
- **To meet legal obligations**: complying with the law, responding to valid legal process, and establishing or defending legal claims.

We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising. The age-gate risk check in Section 5.4 is the only check that scores your device and browser characteristics to decide whether you may use a feature, and it runs only at the age gate.

## 5. Age verification

### 5.1 Declared date of birth

You declare your date of birth when you sign up. Users under 18 cannot use the Services, and an account whose date of birth is under 18 is blocked. A declared date of birth is not, by itself, proof of age.

### 5.2 Optional document check

Some Forges and features require a verified adult. To verify, you can submit a government-issued identity document and, optionally, a selfie holding it.

- **File types.** Each file must be a JPG, PNG, WebP or PDF file of up to 8 MB.
- **Who can see it.** Only staff with admin access can view submitted documents and selfies, and only through the age review queue. They are not public and are not shared with other users.
- **Where it is kept.** Documents are stored on our server's disk, outside the public web folder, and are not linked from any public page.
- **How long it is kept.** A document and any selfie are kept until the review ends. They are deleted when the review is approved, when it is rejected, when you withdraw a pending submission, and when your account is deleted. We do not delete a pending submission on a timer.
- **What is kept after review.** Until your account is deleted, we keep a record of the outcome (approved, rejected or pending), the document type, the date of birth you declared, the review date and the reviewing staff member's account.
- **Withdrawing.** You can withdraw a pending submission at any time while it waits for review.

Identity documents can contain numbers that Texas treats as sensitive personal information under Tex. Bus. & Com. Code ch. 521. We handle them under the controls described in Section 14.

### 5.3 Why we ask

We use the document and any selfie only for the age review. A staff member reviews the submission and decides whether to approve or reject it.

### 5.4 Age-gate checks and device signals

When you confirm your age at the age gate, our server checks the request against risk signals. These include your IP address and user agent, and browser and device characteristics: screen size and colour depth, language, time zone, hardware and memory values, the number of browser plugins, touch support, graphics capability, and whether the browser reports that it is being automated.

The check can refuse or block the confirmation. Each decision is recorded in an age-gate audit record, which includes the IP address and user agent in full. Staff with admin access can review refused and blocked decisions on the admin Age Gate Review page, and can approve a device so that it is allowed through in future. We keep these audit records to review age-gate decisions and to protect the Services.

## 6. How we share personal data

We share personal data only as described below.

### 6.1 Service providers

We give each provider only the information it needs for its function.

- **Stripe** handles payments, subscriptions and the billing portal, and collects your card details directly.
- **Twilio** sends SMS codes for phone verification and two-factor sign-in, where SMS delivery is configured.
- **An email delivery service**, connected to us over SMTP, delivers transactional email, including sign-in and verification codes and security notices.
- **LiveKit** provides real-time media servers that route voice and video in Forge voice and stage channels, where LiveKit is configured. See Section 7.
- **Google** runs public STUN servers. Where the built-in fallback mode described in Section 7 is used, devices contact them to set up connections, and they receive the device's IP address.
- **Relay (TURN) servers**, where configured, carry call traffic in that fallback mode when a direct connection cannot be made.
- **Storage providers.** Uploads are stored in S3-compatible object storage or on our server's storage.
- **Hosting providers** run the Services and our PostgreSQL database.
- **Discord** receives the alerts described in Section 11, and handles any Discord account you link.
- **Browser and operating-system push services** deliver the push notifications you turn on.
- **Link previews.** When a message contains a link, our server visits that page to build a preview. The linked site sees a request from our server, not from you or the people reading the message.

### 6.2 Other users

Other users can see what you share with them, as described in our Terms of Service and your settings. Forge members can see your posts in channels they can access, and your nickname, roles and online status there, unless you hide your status. Files you upload are stored at hard-to-guess addresses, and anyone with the link can open the file.

### 6.3 Law enforcement and legal process

We disclose personal data when we believe in good faith that the law requires it, for example in response to a valid subpoena or court order, or when disclosure is necessary to prevent serious harm. We review each request before responding.

### 6.4 Business transfers

### 6.5 At your direction

### 6.6 No sale and no targeted advertising

We do not sell personal data or process it for targeted advertising.

## 7. Voice, video and screen sharing

### 7.1 What is recorded

We do not record or transcribe voice calls, video or screen sharing. Audio, video and screen content are sent in real time so that the people in the call can hear and see them. Screen content is visible to the people in that call.

### 7.2 How calls are routed

Where LiveKit is configured for the Services, voice and video in Forge voice and stage channels are routed through LiveKit's real-time media servers, which forward audio, video and screen content between the people in the call. Our server gives each participant a token to join the call room, valid for 30 minutes.

If LiveKit is not configured, the Services use a built-in fallback mode. In that mode, participants' devices connect to each other using WebRTC, and the connection is set up through our server's real-time connection. The other people in the call can see your IP address. The public STUN servers operated by Google, which help set up the connection, also receive it. Relay (TURN) servers carry call traffic in that mode when a direct connection cannot be made, where they are configured.

### 7.3 Voiceprints

We do not analyze voice to identify a person, and we do not create voiceprints. See Section 10.

## 8. The desktop app

The Windows desktop app keeps your sign-in session, the browser data described in the Cookie Policy, window and preference settings, update state, logs and any downloaded updates in its data folder on your computer.

Checking for updates sends your IP address and the app version to our website. Updates are downloaded from the location named in the update manifest published there.

## 9. Cookies and local storage

The Services use a small number of cookies and browser storage items, all needed to run the Services. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. The Cookie Policy lists each item by name, purpose, lifetime and controls. In brief:

- **nf_refresh**, an HTTP-only cookie that keeps you signed in. It lasts 30 days by default, or until you sign out.
- **nf_csrf**, a cookie that protects your account against requests sent from other websites. It lasts as long as nf_refresh.
- **The age-gate cookie**, named nexusforge_age18, or __Host-nexusforge_age18 on secure production connections. It records that you confirmed you are 18 or older. It lasts five years and does not contain your date of birth.
- **Browser local and session storage** for your sign-in state, selected Forge, display choices, and voice and video device settings.

## 10. Biometric identifiers

Texas law restricts the capture of biometric identifiers, which the law defines as a retina or iris scan, a fingerprint, a voiceprint, or a record of hand or face geometry. Tex. Bus. & Com. Code § 503.001.

We do not capture or store biometric identifiers as that law defines them.

- **Avatars** are built from preset 3D parts and options, not from photographs or face scans.
- **Selfies** submitted for age verification are viewed by a staff member during the review. Our code does not compare a selfie with the identity document or any other image, and does not create a record of face geometry from it.
- **Voice** is processed in real time during calls. We do not create voiceprints from it.

If we ever decide to capture biometric identifiers, we will first inform you and obtain your consent. We will destroy them within a reasonable time, and no later than the first anniversary of the date on which the purpose for collecting them expires.

## 11. Discord and error alerts

### 11.1 Linking a Discord account

To link a Discord account, you enter a one-time code in our Discord bot. We store your Discord user ID, but not your Discord password. Discord's own privacy policy applies to your Discord account.

### 11.2 Alerts to our team

Our team uses an internal Discord channel to see problems with the Services:

- **Problem reports.** When someone submits a problem report, the first 200 characters of its message are posted to the channel.
- **Server errors.** In production, when a server error occurs, the channel receives a reference code, the request path and the first 300 characters of the error message.
- **Daily summary.** Each morning, the channel receives aggregate figures for the previous 24 hours, such as sign-ups, active people, messages, payments, problem reports and error counts. The summary contains no message content.

Please do not put personal details, passwords or sign-in codes in problem reports, support messages or other messages to us.

## 13. How long we keep personal data

We keep personal data for as long as it is needed for the purposes in Section 4, unless the law requires a longer period.

- **Account information** is kept while your account is active. When you delete your account, deletion is scheduled 14 days later, and you can cancel it during that period by signing in and choosing to keep your account. When deletion is carried out, we replace your username and email address with values that do not identify you, and remove your password, profile details, phone number, date of birth, two-factor settings and recovery codes, sessions, friend and follow connections, Forge memberships, feed posts and comments, notifications and Discord link. Messages you sent in Forges and direct messages remain, shown as sent by a deleted user.
- **Messages and Content** are kept until you or the Forge owner delete them, or they are removed under our policies.
- **Age documents and selfies** are kept as described in Section 5.2.
- **Verification and sign-in codes** are removed one day after they expire. **Discord link codes** are removed when they expire.
- **Session records** are removed one day after they expire, or seven days after they are revoked.
- **Error log entries** are removed 30 days after they were last seen. **Resolved problem reports** are removed 180 days after they are resolved.
- **Read notifications** are removed 90 days after they are created. Other notifications are removed one year after they are created.
- **Admin audit logs and activity logs** are removed one year after they are created.
- **Age-gate audit records** are kept as long as they are needed to review age-gate decisions and to protect the Services.
- **Purchase and Coin transaction records** are kept as long as they are needed for accounting, tax, fraud prevention and legal purposes.
- **Backups.** Deleted data may remain in backup copies until those copies are overwritten.

## 14. Security and breach notification

### 14.1 Our security measures

We use hashed passwords, encrypted authenticator secrets, hashed recovery codes, optional two-factor sign-in, alerts for sign-ins from new devices, the ability to sign out other sessions, rate limiting, and cookies marked secure on production connections. Admin tools are available only to staff accounts with admin permission.

No service can guarantee perfect security. If you believe your account has been compromised, change your password, sign out other sessions and contact us through the Support page.

### 14.2 Breach notification

If a breach of security involves sensitive personal information of Texas residents, we will notify each affected Texas resident without unreasonable delay, and no later than 60 days after we determine that the breach occurred, as required by Tex. Bus. & Com. Code ch. 521. Where a breach affects at least 250 Texas residents, we will also notify the Texas Attorney General as soon as practicable, and no later than 30 days after we determine that the breach occurred. We will notify you by the means the law allows, which may include email to the address on your account.

## 16. Your Texas consumer rights

### 16.1 Your rights

If you are a Texas resident, the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act gives you these rights, subject to its conditions and exceptions. Tex. Bus. & Com. Code § 541.051.

- **Confirm and access.** You can confirm whether we process your personal data, and access it.
- **Correct.** You can ask us to correct inaccurate personal data. You can change most profile details yourself in Settings.
- **Delete.** You can ask us to delete personal data you provided, or that we obtained about you.
- **Portable copy.** You can obtain a copy of the personal data you provided to us, in a portable and, where technically feasible, readily usable format. In the app, go to Settings, then Account, then "Download my data."
- **Opt out.** You can opt out of targeted advertising, the sale of personal data, and profiling that produces a legal or similarly significant effect. We do not engage in targeted advertising or sales. Section 5.4 describes the only check that scores device characteristics.
- **Appeal.** You can appeal a decision we make about a request, as described in Section 16.6.

### 16.2 How to submit a request

Send your request through the Support page at https://nfgcord.com/support, and say that it is a privacy request. Include the username on your account and say which right you want to use. To delete your account, use Settings, then Account, then "Delete my account."

### 16.3 Verifying your identity

We may need to confirm that a request comes from you, using the information we already hold about your account. Never send us your password or sign-in codes to verify a request.

### 16.4 Response time

We respond without undue delay and no later than 45 days after we receive a request. Where reasonably necessary, we may extend that period once, by up to 45 additional days. If we extend it, we will tell you within the first 45 days and explain why.

### 16.5 Fees

We provide information free of charge at least twice a year for each consumer. We may charge a reasonable fee, or decline a request, only if it is manifestly unfounded, excessive or repetitive, and we will explain our reasons.

### 16.6 If we decline a request, and how to appeal

If we decline a request, we will tell you by day 45 why, and how to appeal. To appeal, send your appeal through the Support page at https://nfgcord.com/support, labelled "Appeal," with the original request. Appeal within a reasonable time after you receive our decision. We will respond in writing, with our reasons, no later than 60 days after we receive your appeal. If we deny the appeal, we will tell you how to file a complaint with the Texas Attorney General.

### 16.7 Texas Attorney General

You can file a complaint about how we handle personal data with the Consumer Protection Division of the Texas Attorney General, using the online complaint mechanism on the Attorney General's website.

### 16.8 Limits

We may be unable to provide a feature that depends on data you ask us to delete. We may keep some records where the law requires it, or where they are needed for security, fraud prevention or a legal claim. If we do, we will tell you what we kept and why.
