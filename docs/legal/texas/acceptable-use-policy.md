## 1. About this Policy

**1.1 What this is.** This Acceptable Use Policy ("Policy") sets out what you may and may not do on the Services of Nexus Forge Gaming ("Nexus Forge Gaming," "we," "us," or "our"). It is part of the Terms of Service. Capitalized terms this Policy does not define have the meanings given in the Terms of Service.

**1.2 Definitions.** In this Policy, "**Services**" means the Nexus Forge Gaming web app, the Windows desktop app and everything they offer. "**Forge**" means a community space on the Services, including its channels, threads, events, roles and settings. "**Content**" means anything you post, send, upload, stream or otherwise make available through the Services, including messages, attachments, posts, comments, reactions, emoji, stickers, sounds, profile pictures, banners, avatar designs, event details, and messages sent by bots or webhooks. "**Coins**" means Nexus Forge Coins, the in-app virtual currency described in the Virtual Currency Terms.

**1.3 Who this covers.** This Policy applies to everyone who uses the Services, including Forge owners, moderators and bot developers, and to everything they do on the Services. Where a rule applies more strictly to Forge owners and moderators, it says so.

**1.4 Other documents.** The Community Rules set out everyday expectations for behavior in Forges, voice and events. The Moderation Policy explains how we review reports and decide what to do, and the Appeals Policy explains how to challenge a decision. If this Policy and the Terms of Service conflict, the Terms of Service control, except that the conduct rules in this Policy apply in addition.

**1.5 Age.** The Services are for people aged 18 and over. You must not misstate your age, use another person's age documents, or get around our age checks.

**1.6 Context.** Some conduct is banned outright, including the rules in Section 2.3. Other conduct depends on context, severity and intent, as described in the Moderation Policy. We do not review every message, and nothing in this Policy creates a duty to do so.

## 2. Prohibited conduct

**2.1 Harassment, threats, hate and discrimination.** You must not:

- harass, bully, stalk or intimidate another person, including through repeated unwanted contact or degrading or sexualized comments;
- threaten violence, self-harm or other harm, or encourage someone to hurt themselves;
- attack or demean people because of race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age or another protected characteristic;
- organize brigading, pile-ons or other coordinated campaigns; or
- refuse or limit access to a Forge, channel or event because of a protected characteristic, where you have the power to do so.

**2.2 Doxxing.** You must not publish, share or threaten to publish another person's private information without their consent. This includes real name, home or work address, school, phone number, email address, precise location, government identification, financial information, private photos and IP address. Do not try to learn someone's IP address, for example through a voice or video connection or a link you send. Do not make false emergency reports to send responders to someone's home ("swatting"). Sharing your own information is your choice. Sharing someone else's is not.

**2.3 Sexual content and minors.**

- **(a) Zero tolerance.** You must not post, send, request, share, link to or create any sexual content involving a person under 18. This applies to real, drawn, animated, computer-generated and AI-generated images, video, audio and text that depict minors sexually. There are no exceptions for context, consent or fiction.
- **(b) No sexualizing or grooming.** You must not sexualize a minor in conversation, groom or sexually solicit a minor, ask a minor for photos, contact details, locations or meetings, or pressure anyone to send sexual images (sextortion).
- **(c) Reporting.** When we become aware of apparent child sexual abuse material or related conduct, we remove it, restrict the account involved, and report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) through its CyberTipline and to law enforcement, as the law requires, including under 18 U.S.C. § 2258A. We preserve information only as the law requires. Do not download, save, forward or screenshot such material. Report it instead.
- **(d) Adult sexual content is not allowed.** Adult sexually explicit content is not permitted on the Services in any format. This includes nudity, sex acts, pornography and sexual content made for arousal, in messages, profile pictures, banners, avatars, streams and voice. Non-graphic discussion of sex or relationships is governed by the Community Rules.
- **(e) Intimate images.** You must not share intimate images or video of another person without consent, including manipulated or AI-generated images of a real person, and you must not threaten to share them. Some of this conduct is a crime under Texas law (Tex. Penal Code § 21.16).

**2.4 Violent extremism.** You must not promote, recruit for, raise money for or support terrorist or violent extremist groups, or post their propaganda. Do not praise mass violence or its perpetrators, share attack manifestos or footage of violence against people, or plan or coordinate violence, including in voice channels. Graphic footage of real violence or death, shared to shock or celebrate, is prohibited.

**2.5 Fraud, scams and credential theft.** You must not:

- run or promote scams, including fake giveaways, fake staff or support, fake trades, advance-fee schemes or investment schemes;
- send links, forms or messages that ask for passwords, sign-in codes, recovery codes, card details or other secrets. No one who works for Nexus Forge Gaming should ask you for these, and you should never share them;
- buy, sell, rent, lend or trade accounts, usernames, profiles or access to another person's account. Transferring Forge ownership through the Services' own feature is allowed; or
- gain access to accounts you do not own, including through credential stuffing, keyloggers, session-token theft or "account grabber" software, or pretend to be support or staff to obtain information or access.

**2.6 Malware and security abuse.** Do not share, host or link to malware, trojans, remote-access tools used to control someone else's device, IP loggers, or files and links meant to damage a device or steal data. Do not publish or sell exploit code or attack tools aimed at the Services or other users. If you find a security vulnerability, report it through the Support page at https://nfgcord.com/support, choosing "Something else," and give us a reasonable time to fix it before you disclose it.

**2.7 Spam, raids and automation.** You must not:

- post spam, including repeated messages, unsolicited advertising, link or invite floods, chain messages or mass mentions;
- send unsolicited direct messages or friend requests in bulk;
- organize or join a raid to disrupt a Forge, channel, event or voice room; or
- run self-bots, auto-clickers or scripts on your own account to send messages, join events, react, claim rewards or farm activity.

**2.8 Cheating and the economy.** Coins, mining rigs, the hourly jackpot, cosmetic items and daily rewards are features of the Services. You must not:

- use bots, scripts, multiple accounts, fake activity or any other method to claim rewards, mining income or Coins beyond what the rules allow;
- exploit a bug for Coins, items, jackpot entries or any advantage. Report it to support instead;
- interfere with a jackpot round, its draw or its published seed, or try to manipulate or predict a round's outcome;
- collude with others to abuse Coin transfers, trades or rewards;
- sell, buy or trade Coins, jackpot tickets, cosmetic items or mining rigs for money or anything of value outside the Services. Coins cannot be bought with money and cannot be cashed out or redeemed for money; or
- connect any in-app feature, such as a mining rig, to any outside system, blockchain, exchange or wallet.

The hourly jackpot works like this: players spend Coins on tickets in a round, and one winner receives the pot in Coins. Each round's draw can be checked against its published seed.

Mining rigs are an in-app progression feature with no real-money value. They do not involve cryptocurrency mining. Cosmetic items are a license to use the item inside the Services. They are not property and are not refundable in cash.

The internal in-app assets shown in the Services are not connected to any blockchain or exchange and cannot be withdrawn. Do not offer to exchange them, or Coins, for money or for anything outside the Services.

**2.9 Bots, webhooks and integrations.** Bots, webhooks and integrations must follow this Policy, the Terms of Service and the rules for bot apps and webhooks on the Services. Forge owners are responsible for the bots and webhooks in their Forges. A bot must not impersonate staff or another person, send messages that look like official notices from Nexus Forge Gaming, collect personal information without consent, scrape other users' data, or be used for spam, raids or harassment. Keep bot tokens and webhook URLs secret, because anyone with a webhook URL can post into its channel. Integrations with other services, such as a Discord account link, must also follow those services' rules.

**2.10 Impersonation.** You must not impersonate another person, a brand, a streamer or creator, a Nexus Forge Gaming staff member, a Forge moderator or a bot. Do not use a name, avatar or message that is likely to make people believe you are someone else, or that an official account sent a message. Clearly labeled parody or fan content that does not deceive people is handled under the Community Rules and the Intellectual Property Policy. Online impersonation intended to harm, defraud or intimidate may be a crime under Texas law (Tex. Penal Code § 33.07).

**2.11 Gambling, lotteries and sales.** You must not use the Services to promote, offer or run unlawful gambling, betting or lotteries, including betting on Coin, jackpot or Forge outcomes. Texas law restricts gambling and lotteries (Tex. Penal Code ch. 47; Tex. Const. art. III, § 47). You must not run a prize draw, raffle or giveaway in which a player pays, or gives something of value, to enter. You must not sell or offer to sell Coins, jackpot tickets, accounts or items for money, or advertise such sales.

**2.12 Drugs and weapons.** You must not offer to sell, trade, give away or arrange delivery of illegal drugs, controlled substances (see Tex. Health & Safety Code ch. 481), firearms, ammunition, explosives or other weapons where that would break the law. Do not share instructions for making illegal drugs, explosives or weapons.

**2.13 Circumventing bans, age checks and limits.**

- **Ban evasion.** Do not create or use another account to avoid a suspension, ban, timeout, restriction or Forge ban. Using a VPN or proxy for privacy is not by itself a violation. Using one to evade a ban is.
- **Age checks.** Do not misstate your date of birth, use another person's identity document or photo for age verification, or tamper with the age gate or the age-verification process.
- **Protections.** Do not bypass rate limits, anti-spam controls, AutoMod, CAPTCHAs or other protections, or use tools to hide your activity from them.

**2.14 Attacking the Services.** You must not:

- carry out denial-of-service attacks or flood the Services with traffic;
- scrape or crawl the Services at a rate that burdens them, or collect data about other users at scale, including to build datasets or train machine-learning models, without permission;
- probe or test the Services' security without our written permission, or access systems or data you are not authorized to access (see the federal Computer Fraud and Abuse Act and Tex. Penal Code ch. 33);
- bypass two-factor authentication or other account protections, or attack another person's account; or
- tamper with automatic updates, or reverse engineer the Services except where the End User License Agreement allows it.

**2.15 Voice, video, stages and screen sharing.**

- **No recording or broadcasting without consent.** Do not record, capture, screenshot or broadcast another person's voice, video or screen without their consent. Texas law generally lets a participant in a conversation record it, but intercepting or disclosing communications can be a crime in some circumstances (Tex. Penal Code § 16.02). This Policy is stricter than the law on this point.
- **No sharing of private material.** Do not share another person's private conversation, messages, personal details or private images through voice, video, stages or screen sharing.
- **No streaming others' voice chat.** Do not stream or broadcast a voice room, call or stage outside the Services without the consent of everyone who can be heard.
- **No threats, harassment or disruptive sound.** Do not use voice or video to threaten, harass or sexually pressure anyone, and do not play sounds designed to hurt or disrupt listeners, such as earrape, or use the soundboard to harass people.
- **Respect for minors.** Nothing sexual or romantic may be said or shown to anyone who appears to be under 18. If you believe someone may be under 18, stop, do not ask for their age documents or photos, and report it under Section 2.3.
- **No recording by the Services.** The Services do not record or transcribe voice or video calls.

**2.16 Intellectual property.** Do not post Content you do not have the right to use. Copyright and trademark matters are handled under the Intellectual Property Policy, which explains how to send a notice.

**2.17 Other harmful conduct.** You must not break the law. You must not file reports you know to be false, or use reporting or support tools to harass someone or gain an advantage in a dispute. As a Forge owner or moderator, you must not use moderation tools to harass, target or punish someone, to settle a personal dispute, or for your own advantage. You must not flood support channels or send abusive messages to staff.

## 3. Enforcement

**3.1 How we decide.** We decide under the Moderation Policy. We consider severity, intent, context, harm, your history on the Services, whether the Content was public or private, and whether the account may have been compromised.

**3.2 Enforcement steps.** We usually act in this order:

1. **Warning.** We tell you which rule you broke and what to change. A warning stays on your record.
2. **Content removal.** We or a Forge moderator remove the Content or limit the feature involved.
3. **Temporary restriction.** We limit some features for a set period, such as posting, direct messages, voice, Coin transfers or creating Forges.
4. **Suspension.** You cannot sign in or use the Services for a set period, or until we lift the suspension.
5. **Permanent ban.** We close the account, and you may not create a new one.

**3.3 Skipping steps.** For serious violations we may go straight to suspension or a permanent ban. These include sexual content involving minors, credible threats of violence, doxxing with a threat, coordinated harassment, malware, scams at scale, ban evasion and serious security attacks. We also refer these matters to law enforcement and report them where the law requires.

**3.4 Economy actions.** Where Coins, items, jackpot entries or rewards were obtained through abuse, we may remove them, freeze them during review, or restrict the features that use them, as the Virtual Currency Terms describe.

**3.5 Forge-level actions.** Forge owners and moderators can delete messages, timeout, kick and ban members, and set AutoMod rules, in their own Forge. We may review a Forge action that appears to break this Policy or the Community Rules, and we may act against a Forge owner or moderator who misuses their powers.

**3.6 Emergencies.** If there is an imminent risk of serious harm, we may act before a review is complete, by removing Content, restricting an account or disabling a feature. We review each such action as soon as practicable under the Moderation Policy.

**3.7 Notice and appeals.** We tell you what action we took and why, unless the law or a safety concern requires otherwise. You can appeal under the Appeals Policy. Repeated violations generally lead to stronger action.

## 4. Reporting

**4.1 How to report.** Use Report on a message or on a profile. Reports about channel messages go to that Forge's moderators, and Nexus Forge Gaming staff can also review them. Reports about direct messages go to Nexus Forge Gaming staff. If you cannot sign in, use the Report a problem form on the Support page at https://nfgcord.com/support. For any other concern, open a support ticket from the Support page and choose the closest category.

**4.2 Reasons.** Choose the closest reason: Spam, Harassment, Hate, Sexual, Violence, Self-harm, Scam or Other. You can add a short description.

**4.3 Urgent situations.** If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first. In the United States, you can call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Then report the concern to us.

**4.4 Good faith.** Reporting in good faith is encouraged. Abusing the reporting system breaks Section 2.17.
