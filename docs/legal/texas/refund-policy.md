## 1. About this policy

1.1 This Refund Policy explains what you buy from Nexus Forge Gaming, when you receive it, how subscriptions renew and cancel, when we refund a purchase, and how to ask for one. It covers Core+ subscriptions, add-ons and cosmetic items bought through the Services.

1.2 It is read together with the Terms of Service, the Virtual Currency Terms, the Privacy Policy (which covers payment information), the Acceptable Use Policy, the Moderation Policy and the Appeals Policy. Defined terms in the Terms of Service apply here, including "Nexus Forge Gaming" (also written "Forge"), "we", "us", "you", "Services", "Content", "Coins" and "Core+". A "forge" in lower case is a community space within the Services.

1.3 The operator is TRH Development, a sole proprietorship owned and operated by Hank. It is not a separate legal entity.

## 2. What you can buy

2.1 **Core+ subscriptions.** Core+ is sold in four plans: Core, Plus, Elite and Infinite. Each plan has different limits and features, such as higher upload limits, more forges and better video quality. The features of each plan are shown on our pricing page.

2.2 **Billing periods.** Core+ is billed monthly or yearly. The price and billing period are shown on the pricing page before you buy.

2.3 **Add-ons.** Add-ons are extra paid features sold through the same billing system. Their prices are shown before you buy.

2.4 **Coins and cosmetic items.** Coins are an in-app virtual currency. You cannot buy Coins with money. Cosmetic items (outfits, accessories and emotes) are bought with Coins.

2.5 **When you receive it.** Core+ and add-ons start when our payment provider confirms your payment and your account is updated. Cosmetic items are added to your account when you spend the Coins.

## 3. Taxes

3.1 Sales and use tax may apply to some digital purchases in Texas. Where tax applies, a refund of the taxed amount follows the same rules as the price.

## 4. Subscription billing and renewal

4.1 Core+ subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period, at the price in effect at renewal, until you cancel.

4.2 We will tell you before a price change applies to your subscription.

4.3 Payments are processed by Stripe. Stripe charges your payment method for each renewal. We do not store your full card number.

## 5. Cancelling a subscription

5.1 You can cancel in **Settings > Billing**.

5.2 Cancelling stops future renewals. Cancelling does not charge you again.

5.3 Your access continues until the end of the billing period you have already paid for. After that, your account returns to the free plan.

5.4 Cancelling does not refund the period that you have already paid for. A refund may be available under section 6.

5.5 You must cancel any active subscription before you can delete your account, as the Privacy Policy explains.

## 6. Refunds for Core+ and add-ons

6.1 We review each refund request for a Core+ subscription or an add-on case by case. This policy does not set a fixed number of days in which a request must be made.

6.2 When we review a request, we consider when you bought the item, whether you have used the paid benefits, and any other facts you give us.

6.3 This applies to renewal charges too. A request about a renewal charge is reviewed case by case in the same way.

6.4 A request for a refund of the unused part of a billing period is also reviewed case by case. We do not promise a pro-rated refund.

6.5 Nothing in this section limits any refund that the law requires, or any refund under section 10 for a duplicate charge or a purchase that was not delivered.

## 7. Add-ons

7.1 Requests for add-ons follow section 6.

7.2 If we stop offering an add-on that you bought, we will tell you what happens to it. We will consider any refund request for it case by case.

## 8. Coins

8.1 Coins have no cash value. They cannot be bought with money, cashed out, sold or redeemed for money. Because of this, there is no Coin purchase to refund. Coins are not refundable in cash. The Virtual Currency Terms describe how Coins work.

8.2 Coins you spend on in-app items are final and are not refundable in cash. This includes cosmetic items, mining rigs and jackpot entries. Mining rigs are an in-app progression feature that adds Coins over time. They have no real-money value and do not involve cryptocurrency mining.

8.3 If a jackpot round is cancelled, for example because too few players joined, the Coins used for your entries are returned to you in Coins.

8.4 Where the transfer feature allows it, Coins you send to another user are not refundable.

8.5 If we make an error that costs you Coins, we will correct it by returning Coins, not cash.

## 9. Cosmetic items

9.1 Cosmetic items are bought with Coins. Once you own an item, you have a licence to use it inside the Services, as the Terms of Service describe. Items are not property. Cosmetic items are not refundable in cash after they are used, except where the law requires a refund.

9.2 If a cosmetic item is removed from the store, the items you already own stay in your account.

## 10. Duplicate charges and purchases that were not delivered

10.1 If you are charged twice for the same purchase, or you are charged and the purchase is not delivered, contact Support. We will refund the duplicate or failed charge in full, whether or not any time has passed since the purchase.

## 11. Chargebacks and payment disputes

11.1 Please contact Support before you file a chargeback or payment dispute with your bank or card issuer. We would like the chance to fix the problem first. Most issues can be resolved quickly.

11.2 You have the right to dispute a charge with your bank or card issuer. Filing a dispute does not, on its own, end your account.

11.3 If a dispute is open, we will tell you if any benefit linked to the disputed payment is paused while it is investigated, and we will explain how it is restored.

11.4 If a dispute is filed, we may send the bank or payment provider the purchase and account records that it asks for. Those records are handled as the Privacy Policy describes.

## 12. How to request a refund

12.1 Open **Support** in the Services and create a ticket in the **"Coins & purchases"** category. You can also use the Support page at https://nfgcord.com/support.

12.2 Include your username, the date of the purchase, the plan or item, and your reason for asking. Never send your password or sign-in codes. Do not send full card numbers.

12.3 We review each request case by case, and we reply as soon as we can.

12.4 An approved refund goes back to the original payment method through Stripe. How long it takes to appear depends on your bank or card issuer.

12.5 If we decide not to refund a purchase, we will explain why in the ticket. If the refund decision is part of an account action, you may use the Appeals Policy.

## 13. Discontinued features and account actions

13.1 If we discontinue a paid feature, we will tell you in the Services and explain your options, which may include a refund or a Coin remedy.

13.2 If we suspend or close an account for breaking the Terms of Service, the Acceptable Use Policy or the Community Rules, a refund is not automatic. Any refund follows the law and the Moderation Policy.

13.3 You can appeal an account decision under the Appeals Policy.

## 14. Your rights under Texas and federal law

14.1 Nothing in this Refund Policy limits any right that you have under the Texas Deceptive Trade Practices Act, Tex. Bus. & Com. Code § 17.41 et seq., or any other law, where that right cannot be waived. Waivers of rights under that Act are governed by Tex. Bus. & Com. Code § 17.42.

14.2 Federal rules on online subscriptions and automatic renewal, including the Restore Online Shoppers' Confidence Act, 15 U.S.C. §§ 8401 to 8405, and the Federal Trade Commission's Negative Option Rule, 16 C.F.R. part 425, may apply to Core+ billing. Sections 4 and 5 describe how billing and cancellation work.

14.3 If you have a complaint about a purchase, you may contact the Consumer Protection Division of the Texas Attorney General.
